## Implement

1. Refine distinct procedural SVG paths for the six approved silhouette/color pairs. Use timed word IDs and DOM rects, group line runs deterministically, and coordinate their overlapping shapes behind text. Re-measure after width/font/placement changes via a throttled observer. Animate geometry and color while keeping `annotation_id` stable across revision/history. Use the supplied opacity and smooth intensity-driven size/saturation, with gentle 2–4 s breathing and roughly 250–450 ms transitions.
2. Draw two-emotion gradients and blend silhouette terms only when the backend supplies a valid blend. Act on retraction by fading out the draft. Keep cloud layers pointer-transparent. Respect reduced motion with a brief non-looping transition and retain visual meaning. Avoid cloud glows or overlap that undermine text.
3. Add an inspectable target per phrase, keyboard focus support plus hover/click/tap. Show category, relative support, all nine scores, and the TDD's caveat (“Model scores (not calibrated probabilities)”). Make it dismissible and readable without color alone. Radar, timeline and translation are outside this prompt.

Assert word bounds and lineRuns before and after a resize, font/layout change, and center→history move. Check keyboard focus, touch activation, pointer transparency and contrast.
