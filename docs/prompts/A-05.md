## Implement

1. Classify B's upstream failures. Retry transient failures with bounded exponential backoff and jitter (start at 0.5 s, cap at 4 s and configured attempts). Preserve committed text, clear only stale partial, and anchor a fresh upstream epoch at the first forwarded sample. Stop retrying for matching auth, quota and terms failures. On exhausted retry send a fatal state and preserve the frontend transcript.
2. Keep text-only work live when Large fails or is too slow. Surface a useful nonblocking warning, observe queue drops, and avoid replaying old mic frames. Do not alter finalized annotations or attach late results to a new session.
3. Enforce bounded Stop drain and Pause/Resume cleanup. Fence model results by session/epoch. Ensure leases, tasks and sockets close on Stop, fatal error, disconnect, and shutdown. Preserve prior UI data against recoverable failures. Reset clears all ephemeral session state. Keep debug metrics content-free.
