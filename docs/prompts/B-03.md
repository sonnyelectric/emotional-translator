## Implement

1. Export `ElevenLabsScribeAdapter(*, api_key, vad_silence_secs)` for A's independent composition. Use the TDD endpoint and `model_id=scribe_v3_live`, `audio_format=pcm_16000`, `commit_strategy=vad`, and `include_timestamps=true`. Authenticate with backend `xi-api-key` and source config from A. Send PCM as documented provider chunk JSON. Verify the VAD silence parameter in preflight before relying on the value. Keep connect and close bounded and cancellation-safe.
2. Translate provider partial, commit, and timed events to the Scribe notices without assigning canonical word IDs or guessing a timestamp basis. Keep emitted order and proven provider keys. Classify auth, quota and terms as fatal, network/rate limit/transient as retryable, and drop keepalive noise.
3. Implement an opt-in diagnostic that sends a permitted local mono 16 kHz PCM sample at live pace, or accepts PCM from an explicitly supplied stdin source. It prints scrubbed metadata only. Verify actual ordering, timed-word payload and time basis over multiple commits and a second fresh stream, and document unsupported or ambiguous observations. The sample is optional diagnostics, not part of app setup or submission.
