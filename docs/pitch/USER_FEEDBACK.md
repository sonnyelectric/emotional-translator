# User feedback: 3-minute interview script (2 to 3 attendees, today)

Goal: real feedback for the "user evidence" score and the submission. Never invent or embellish answers. Write what they say in their words. Source for questions: `docs/research/R3` s7. Framing rules: R3 s2 (ask autistic or Deaf attendees before saying anything about them on stage).

## Who to ask
Anyone who reads captions or has thought about tone in text-only communication. Do not assume anyone's hearing or neurotype. Never pressure. If someone says no, thank them and stop.

## 0:00 Consent line (read aloud)
"I am building a captioning tool and would like three minutes of honest feedback. I will take notes. I would like to quote you, anonymously unless you say otherwise. You can say no, skip any question, or stop any time. Is that okay?"
Record: consent Y/N, and whether they allow a named quote (Y/N). No audio recording unless they explicitly agree.

## 0:20 Q1 (before the demo)
"Think of the last time you read captions in a meeting or video. Was there a moment you could not tell how someone meant what they said? What happened next?"

## 1:00 Demo (30 seconds, no explanation)
Show one angry and one sad line. Say nothing about what the shapes mean.

## 1:30 Q2
"What do you think this cloud is telling you? What would you do with it?"

## 2:00 Q3
"When would you want this on, and when would you turn it off? Who should never be able to see it?"

## 2:30 Q4 (pick one if time is short)
"Where would this get it wrong, or make you trust it too much?" or "If it is wrong half the time, is it still useful? What would it need to show so you know how sure it is?"

## 3:00 Close
"Thank you. May I quote one line, and how should I describe you (for example 'a student')?"

## Do and do not
- Do not lead ("Isn't this useful?"). Do not explain the shapes before Q2.
- Do not claim or imply any attendee's feedback was an endorsement.
- If an answer is negative, record it exactly. Negative feedback goes in the submission too.
- Do not record names, audio or faces unless permitted.
